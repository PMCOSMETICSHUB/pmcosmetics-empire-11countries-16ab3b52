This directory is reserved for verified PM-owned real product images (72 expected images). An image may be staged only after its source is confirmed as an actual PM-owned product image and its identity is matched to the relevant product record.

Do not add placeholders, mockups, stock images, generated illustrations, or guessed images.

## Required evidence for each image

1. Source archive or original file supplied by the owner.
2. Product SKU association confirmed against the product master.
3. File type and dimensions inspected.
4. SHA-256 hash recorded from the actual file.
5. Ownership/source provenance documented.
6. No placeholder or synthetic content.

Image staging alone does not open the commercial publication gate. Product publication remains subject to the separate product evidence, stock, supplier, cost, and approval checks.
